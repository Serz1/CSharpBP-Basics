# AISoulGuide Brand Guidelines

## Brand Identity

**The Awakened Architect** - Bridging technology and spirituality for conscious transformation.

### Core Positioning
- **Not** "woo-woo" spirituality
- **Not** cold technology
- **Integration** of both: practical spirituality for modern humans
- **Shadow work emphasis** - differentiator from toxic positivity

### Mission
Guide seekers to awaken through the integration of divine wisdom and systematic thinking.

**Sizes:**

- Section Headers: 24-28pt

**Font:** Philosopher Bold

**Font:** Lora Regular
**Size:** 10-12pt
**Usage:** Readable, slightly literary feel

**Font:** Philosopher Italic
**Size:** 11-14pt
**Usage:** Distinguishes prompts from body text

### Quotes
**Font:** Philosopher Italic
**Size:** 12-16pt
**Usage:** The Architect's wisdom, inspirational text

## Page Layout Specifications

### Planner/Journal Interior Page
**Size:** 8.5" × 11" (US Letter) or A4
**Margins:** 0.5" all sides
**Safe Zone:** 0.25" from edge (no critical text/elements)

**Header Design:**
```
Height: 1" (96px at 96dpi)
Left: Small cosmic icon (✨)
Center: Page title (Playfair Display, 24px)
Right: Date field (Lora, 14px)
Bottom: Thin line (#4DD0E1, 1px)
```

**Writing Space:**
```
Lines: 0.3" spacing (28.8px)
Color: #E5E5E5 (light gray)
Prompt: Lora Italic, 16px, #4A2C5E
Area: 6-7 inches height
```

**Footer:**
```
Height: 0.5" (48px)
Left: ✨ AISoulGuide.com (Lora, 10px, #888)
Right: Page number (Lora, 10px, #888)
Bottom Decoration: Small mandala, #8B5CF6 at 20% opacity
```

### Card Designs
**Size:** 3.375" × 2.125" (credit card size)
**Format:** Two-sided (front + back)
**Bleed:** 1/8" for easier cutting

**Angel Numbers Quick Guide Card:**
- Background: Cosmic gradient (midnight to deep purple)
- Border: Golden glow (#FCEEAC)
- Numbers: Celestial gold, bold
- Stars: Scattered decorative elements

## Brand Voice

### Shadow Awakening Voice
**Tone:** Direct, confrontational, activating
**Style:** Short sentences, bold statements, truth bombs
**Example:** "You're not broken. You're WAKING UP. It's supposed to hurt."

### Luminous Harmony Voice
**Tone:** Nurturing, supportive, gentle
**Style:** Flowing sentences, affirmations, encouragement
**Example:** "Your awakening is unfolding perfectly. Trust the gentle unfoldment of your soul."

### The Architect Voice
**Tone:** Wise, bridging, systems-thinking
**Style:** Both/and thinking, integration, pragmatic spirituality
**Example:** "Shadow AND light. Technology AND soul. You contain multitudes. Integration is the path."

## Usage Guidelines

### Do's
✅ Use sacred geometry as subtle watermarks
✅ Maintain high contrast for readability
✅ Include both shadow and light elements
✅ Use cosmic imagery (galaxies, stars, nebulae)
✅ Keep The Architect's signature consistent
✅ Balance mystical with practical

### Don'ts
❌ Use only light/positive imagery (toxic positivity)
❌ Make text unreadable over busy backgrounds
❌ Use Comic Sans or playful fonts
❌ Ignore the dark/shadow aspects
❌ Make it look "cheap" or amateur
❌ Copy other spiritual brands exactly
